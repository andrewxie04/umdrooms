# Antonov circulation evidence review — 2026-10-03

## Supported observations

- [HDR / UMD building guide](https://www.cs.umd.edu/sites/default/files/images/cs50/cs_day_program_book_v2.pdf), Level 1 spread, printed pages 8–9: curved front circulation with stairs; a south foyer with transverse paired doors; an opening at the foyer end toward the Family Garden. Inspected PDF page index 8 at 2× and enlarged crops at 5×/9×. Preserve page origin when tracing these features. The plan alone does not establish the opening's elevation or the function of every stair landing door.
- [HDR interior photograph](https://www.hdrinc.com/portfolio/brendan-iribe-center-computer-science-and-engineering): the rear brick wall and broad windows are directly visible from the seating room. A separate enclosed passage must not replace these rear windows.
- [UMD garden photographs](https://arboretum.umd.edu/margulis-antonov-family-garden): curved brick, broad dark glazing, gray deck, cream planters and slatted benches. These images do not establish which panes are doors or show the transverse foyer doors clearly.

## Unresolved geometry

The seating now follows the guide's three fanned banks, 298 drawn chair symbols and wider middle cross-aisle; see [the seating trace review](antonov-seating-2026-10-03.md). Its registration into the existing enclosure, metric spacing, 5.5 m rear platform and 6.5 m garden remain estimates. They do not establish the elevation of the south seating doorway. The front stair and foyer must be fitted to this seating arrangement with their relative heights explicitly documented. A tested prototype extending an enclosed passage around the entire rear wall was discarded after comparison with the architect photograph. No prototype passage, altered garden perimeter or invented rear stair remains in the site.

New primary photographs by Nick Seitz show the front stair outside the auditorium, continuing beside the copper upper volume toward the roof collar. Its three flights and landings are now modeled with provisional elevations; see [the exterior stair review](antonov-exterior-stair-2026-10-03.md). The complete upper access continuation, the south foyer, room assignment of the recessed landing door and their confirmed elevations remain incomplete. A legible as-built section through these areas, or a floor plan with spot elevations, would resolve their relative heights. Exact foyer and door finishes also need photographic evidence.

## Additional references and their limits

- [Mahan Rykiel project](https://www.mahanrykiel.com/portfolio/umcp-computer-sciences/) and its [preliminary section](https://www.mahanrykiel.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/UMCP.jpg): stacked auditorium-like spaces and a planted terrace. Room names, cut location and a transferable datum are absent; the printed elevations were not assigned to the model.
- [Official UMD architect panel, 47:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dLYgBlzL-C4&t=2820s): discussion of a stairwell, skylight and curved wall. The inspected frame shows the panel rather than the stair corner, so it does not verify the modeled route.

Photographs were inspected as references and are not redistributed as site textures. Metric dimensions and finish colors remain visual estimates.

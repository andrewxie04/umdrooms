# UMD science and engineering building references

## Evidence and modeling rules

- **ID authority:** `public/campus-data.json` supplies every `umdCode`, OSM ID, outer footprint, and mapped multipolygon hole. The 15 code–ID pairs below were checked directly against that file. The OSM outline is geographic evidence; it does not supply exact facade layout or floor-to-floor heights.
- **Image evidence:** UMD Facilities building inventory photographs and UMD department/project photographs support the visible material and entry descriptions below. A photograph of one elevation is not evidence for the entire roof.
- **Roof reference:** the local `design-reference/campus-aerial-core.jpg` and `campus-aerial-wide.jpg` are **design references only** from [Maryland MD iMAP SixInchImagery](https://mdgeodata.md.gov/imagery/rest/services/SixInch/SixInchImagery/ImageServer). Their Prince George's County imagery is from 2023, so it predates the completed 2024 Chemistry Wing 1. No aerial is rendered or bundled in the scene.
- **Approximation convention:** all model heights, roof unit sizes, window spacing, entry offsets, and colors are stylized campus-scale approximations unless a source explicitly gives them. The model uses native Three.js geometry and `helpers.withColor` for merge compatibility. Flat dark roof planes and generic small service enclosures are inferred from the reference aerial, not documented architectural specifications.

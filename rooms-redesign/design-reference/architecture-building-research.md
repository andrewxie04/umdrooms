# Architecture Building reference notes

- [UMD Facilities Management, building 145](https://facilities.umd.edu/node/372): the official exterior photograph shows dark brown brick, angled roof forms, a recessed glazed band, and a low brick wall along the Campus Drive approach. Its inventory records 1972 construction.
- [UMD School of Architecture, Great Space](https://arch.umd.edu/student-experience/spaces-and-studio/great-space): the heart of the building is a two-story atrium flanked by studios.
- [UMD School of Architecture, Inhabit magazine](https://arch.umd.edu/sites/default/files/2026-02/Inhabit_Magazine_2022_rem.pdf): describes the existing sawtooth roof.

The model keeps the mapped footprint and existing shed-roof rhythm, then adds the brick screen, recessed glazing, and approach wall visible in the exterior photo. Dimensions are approximate where measured elevations were unavailable.

# Cambridge Community residence halls

UMD [Resident Life](https://reslife.umd.edu/explore-halls/residence-halls/cambridge-community) lists five halls in the Cambridge Community. Their individual [Residential Facilities](https://drf.umd.edu/facilities/residence-halls-communities) pages supply exterior photographs and floor counts:

| Hall | Official reference | Visible traits used in the model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bel Air | [Building photo and record](https://drf.umd.edu/facilities/residence-halls-communities/bel-air-hall) | Four-story warm brick bar, light rectangular doorway surround, regular white-framed windows. |
| Cambridge | [Building photo and record](https://drf.umd.edu/facilities/residence-halls-communities/cambridge-hall) | Four-story long brick bar, high pale four-column entrance, regular tall windows. |
| Centreville | [Building photo and record](https://drf.umd.edu/facilities/residence-halls-communities/centreville-hall) | Nine-story dark brick tower with a regular window rhythm and small pale ground entry. |
| Chestertown | [Building photo and record](https://drf.umd.edu/facilities/residence-halls-communities/chestertown-hall) | Four-story brick bar with a pale, framed central entry. |
| Cumberland | [Building photo and record](https://drf.umd.edu/facilities/residence-halls-communities/cumberland-hall) | Nine-story brick tower, pale columned entrance and broad entry steps. |

The native Three.js models retain each mapped footprint and tagged height. They use two massing families, separate brick tones, roof coping and entry treatments. Day and night window placement now follows the official four- or nine-floor count for these halls. The exact entrance edge, column dimensions, rooftop equipment and facade colors are approximations from photos and the mapped footprint, not measured drawings. Source photographs are used only as references and are not drawn on the map.

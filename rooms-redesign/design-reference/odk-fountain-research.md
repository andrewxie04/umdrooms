# ODK Fountain reference notes

- [UMD Omicron Delta Kappa fountain history](https://odk.umd.edu/odk-fountain) identifies the fountain on McKeldin Mall and its five terraces, representing five areas of leadership.
- [UMD Campus Attractions](https://cvs.umd.edu/campus-attractions) describes the fountain as 16 feet by 250 feet and places a sundial nearby. The published dimensions are approximate; the existing OSM water polygon is longer and narrower in the local projection, so the model follows the mapped footprint for alignment with paths.
- UMD's fountain photograph on the ODK page shows low pale stone coping and long shallow water basins descending eastward from the McKeldin Library end of the mall.

The native Three.js model divides the mapped water polygon into five shallow stepped basins. Pale stone edges, cross-weirs, and narrow water lips make the tiers readable from the map's normal camera height. Exact elevations, coping widths, and water colors are visual estimates; no measured construction drawings were found. The source photograph and campus aerial are references only and do not render in the site.

# Lee Building and Turner Hall visual references

These native Three.js models use the campus-data footprints for placement and UMD photographs for facade details. Heights, hidden elevations, and roof depth are estimated because the public pages do not provide measured drawings.

## Lee Building (OSM way/23891545)

- [UMD Facilities building record](https://facilities.umd.edu/node/319): constructed in 1969; administrative use.
- [UMD Facilities front photograph](https://facilities.umd.edu/sites/default/files/UMDBuildings/071.jpg): long two-story red brick wings, regularly spaced white sash windows, low gray roof with small dormers, broad entrance stairs, and a central white pediment carried by **four** tall columns.
- The model follows the long mapped footprint, including the entrance projection. Roof pitches, heights, exact bay spacing, and the rear elevation are visual approximations.

## Turner Hall Visitor Center (OSM way/23989098)

- [UMD Facilities building record](https://facilities.umd.edu/node/324): constructed in 1923, renovated in 1964.
- [UMD Facilities front photograph](https://facilities.umd.edu/sites/default/files/UMDBuildings/079.jpg) and [UMD Conferences & Visitor Services photograph](https://cvs.umd.edu/sites/default/files/inline-images/TurnerHall_05102012_4021%20%28Large%29.jpg): red brick stepped gable with two square high piers and a round opening, multi-story white sash windows, and a long open white entrance canopy. A lower side wing has arched openings.
- [UMD visitor services contact page](https://cvs.umd.edu/about-us/contact-us) identifies the Welcome Desk at Turner Hall.
- The model follows the oblique mapped footprint. The principal wall direction is inferred from its long edges; gable proportions, roof shape, secondary elevations, and canopy dimensions are visual approximations. The lower wing's exact massing remains simplified.

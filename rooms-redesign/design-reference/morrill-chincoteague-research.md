# Morrill and Chincoteague Hall references

## Morrill Hall — OSM `way/24306090`

The [UMD Facilities building page](https://facilities.umd.edu/node/295) provides a current east-front photograph. The [Maryland Historical Trust inventory](https://www.mncppcapps.org/planning/HistoricCommunitiesSurvey/Documentations/PG%2066-035-06%20Morrill%20Hall/PG%2066-035-06%20Morrill%20Hall%20MIHP.pdf), pages 2–3, describes a Second Empire brick building: three stories, seven front bays, six side bays, a straight-sided slate mansard, a four-story square east entrance tower, pedimented dormers, two brick chimneys, pale window sills and lintels, and a central entrance stair. The model follows the mapped 17 × 24 m rectangular footprint; the raised tower, mansard roof, dormers and window rhythm are distinct from the surrounding Georgian campus buildings. The aerial footprint is an outline, so detailed projections and facade spacing are scaled interpretations of the architectural description and current photograph.

## Chincoteague Hall — OSM `way/23546179`

The [UMD Facilities building page](https://facilities.umd.edu/node/308) identifies it as a 1957 academic building and provides a front photograph. The photograph shows a long red-brown brick building with three visible levels, a raised central white four-column portico, triangular pediment, three arched openings at ground level, a balcony rail, broad front stairs, a slate roof and two groups of small white dormers. The model uses the mapped 41 × 19 m footprint. The northward central projection of that footprint locates the portico; column count, arch count, dormer groupings and stair direction follow the UMD image. Precise heights and bay positions are visual approximations.

# Oakland and Prince Frederick residence halls

The official [Oakland Hall](https://drf.umd.edu/facilities/residence-halls-communities/oakland-hall) page shows two red-brick wings framing a cream elevator/stair bay. Its facade has regularly spaced light-framed windows, a pale ground register, a dark rooftop volume over the center, and nine documented floors. The model keeps the bent, U-like mapped footprint and places the stair bay on the long wall inside the court. Its 26.4 m body height comes from the imported campus data; 29 m includes the roof volume and is not a measured survey height.

The official [Prince Frederick Hall](https://drf.umd.edu/facilities/residence-halls-communities/prince-frederick-hall) page shows a brick L-shaped complex with a pale ground level, tall recessed glass lobby bay, grey vertical window strips, and a projecting entrance canopy. The page documents seven floors and north and south entrances. The model follows the mapped L-shaped footprint, includes glazed ground-floor bays and both entrances, and retains its imported 26.4 m height. Bay placement and facade spacing are visual interpretations of the published photo, not an architectural survey.

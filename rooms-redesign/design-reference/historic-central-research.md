# Historic / central building references

These 14 landmark models use native Three.js geometry only. No reference image is loaded by the site. The current building footprints and mapped courtyard holes come from `public/campus-data.json`. The aerial used **only for roof and plan reference** is `design-reference/campus-aerial-core.jpg`, whose service URL, bounds and 2023 imagery date are recorded in `design-reference/campus-aerial.SOURCE.txt` ([Maryland MD iMAP imagery service](https://mdgeodata.md.gov/imagery/rest/services/SixInch/SixInchImagery/ImageServer)).

**Evidence labels:** “Verified” below means visible in the linked UMD Facilities photograph or stated on its building page. “Aerial observation” means visually discernible in the local georeferenced aerial; it is not a measured architectural drawing. All meter heights, roof pitches, window bay counts, colors, portico depths and small details in the models are **approximate** visual abstractions. Pale trim is modeled as a color, not asserted to be limestone or any other specific material. The UMD campus survey describes the wider historic core's brick, symmetrical facades, porticoes and mixed hipped/gabled roofs, but those general traits were not taken as proof for any individual building: [Prince George's County campus architectural survey, p. 3](https://www.mncppcapps.org/planning/HistoricCommunitiesSurvey/CommunityDocumentations/66-035%20University%20of%20Maryland%2C%20College%20Park/PG%2066-035%20University%20of%20Maryland%2C%20College%20Park%20Community%20Survey.pdf).

The photo views establish facade treatment, but some entrance directions are inferred from each footprint, campus paths and aerial orientation. Hornbake's six-column west portico now includes its recessed glazed entrance, pale name band, shallow steps and exhibit banners visible in the [Facilities photograph](https://facilities.umd.edu/sites/default/files/styles/optimized/public/UMDBuildings/147.jpg?itok=zGm_lv3B). Banner colors and positions are approximate. Other models intentionally omit inscriptions, sculptures, exact window muntins and roof equipment whose form could not be established reliably from these references.

The 3D selection camera now approaches Hornbake from the west, across Hornbake Plaza, so its modeled columned main entrance is visible on both phone and desktop. The [UMD Career Center walking directions](https://careers.umd.edu/about-us/visit) place that colonnade beside the plaza and distinguish it from the South Wing entrance. The approach bearing is a presentation choice, not a surveyed facade orientation.

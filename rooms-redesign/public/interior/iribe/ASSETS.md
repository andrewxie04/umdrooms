# Iribe interior daylight asset

`daylight-sky-1k.hdr` is the 1024×512 HDR version of **Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)**, by Greg Zaal (original) and Jarod Guest (sky edit), from Poly Haven.

- Original MD5: `c69498687e876bf68d2a8b8a62234eb9`

The original HDR asset is retained unchanged. It is an illustrative daylight sky, not a photograph of UMD or a current-weather simulation. The interior uses its photographed clouds and a prefiltered ambient/reflection copy, with matching background/environment rotation and sun azimuth. The local file loads only when entering the building and is disposed on exit. The lighting copy caps solar radiance because the scene also has a directional sun; the background preserves the original HDR range.
